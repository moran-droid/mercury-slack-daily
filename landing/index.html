Message & Meta compliance

Say it this way, not that way.

Meta rejects ads that promise specific returns, imply get-rich-quick outcomes, or claim knowledge of someone's finances. Left column is safe to run; right column gets flagged.

✓ Safe to run

“Acquiring a business can be a stronger use of capital than paid ads.”

✕ Gets flagged

“Guaranteed 10X ROAS — double your business, guaranteed.”

✓ Safe to run

“We help founders source and structure acquisitions in their space.”

✕ Gets flagged

“Give us $100k and get rich buying businesses.”

✓ Safe to run

“For B2B owners exploring acquisition as a growth channel.”

✕ Gets flagged

“Struggling with your ad results? Fix your finances now.”

✓ Safe to run

Qualifiers: “illustrative,” “results vary,” “subject to lender approval.”

✕ Gets flagged

Absolutes: “guaranteed,” “risk-free,” “you will,” exact income figures.
